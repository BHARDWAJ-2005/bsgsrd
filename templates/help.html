👦 Boys (Shirt & Pant)
  • Height: Measured in centimeters (cm)
  • Chest: Across fullest part of chest (inches)
  • Waist: Normal pant waistband line (inches)
  • Neck: Base of neck collar size (inches)
  • Shoulder: Tip to tip across back (inches)
  • Sleeve: Shoulder point to wrist (inches)
  • Shirt Length & Pant Length: Full length in inches
👧 Girls (Kameez & Salwar/Skirt)
  • Height: Measured in centimeters (cm)
  • Bust: Around fullest part of chest (inches)
  • Waist: Natural waist circumference (inches)
  • Hip: Around fullest part of hips (inches)
  • Shoulder & Sleeve: In inches
  • Kameez Length: Shoulder down to knee (inches)
  • Salwar/Skirt: Waistband down to ankle/knee (inches)
